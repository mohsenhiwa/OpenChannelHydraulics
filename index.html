Core theoretical foundations, derivations, and practical design principles for open channel flow engineering.

Uniform Flow & Critical Flow Conditions

Derivation of Chézy and Manning formulae; hydraulic radius definitions; section factor for uniform flow; critical depth criteria where Froude number $Fr = 1$; minimum specific energy derivations for rectangular and trapezoidal flumes.

$$Q = \frac{1}{n} A R^{2/3} S_0^{1/2} \quad \text{and} \quad \frac{Q^2 B}{g A^3} = 1$$
Module 2 Sheet E2

Rapidly Varied Flow: Transitions & Hydraulic Jumps

Specific energy diagram application over upward bed steps ($\Delta z$) and channel contractions ($B_2 < B_1$). Choking phenomena, choking limits ($B_{\text{min}}$ and $\Delta z_{\text{crit}}$), momentum conservation, Bélanger jump equation, and energy dissipation.

$$y_2 = \frac{y_1}{2}\left(\sqrt{1 + 8Fr_1^2} - 1\right), \quad \Delta E = \frac{(y_2 - y_1)^3}{4 y_1 y_2}$$

Gradually Varied Flow & Numerical Integration

Gradually Varied Flow (GVF) dynamic differential equation; classification of surface profiles (M1, M2, M3, S1, S2, S3); upstream vs downstream controls; Direct Step and Standard Step numerical discretisation techniques.

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{S_0 - S_f}{1 - Fr^2}, \quad \Delta x = \frac{\Delta E_s}{\overline{S_0 - S_f}}$$

Long Channel Transitions & Jump Locations

Coupled reach transitions across mild and steep slopes; determination of control depth locations; hydraulic jump sequencing between steep-to-mild and mild-to-steep channel junctions.

Section E5: Relevant Equations of Steady Open Channel Flow

Official reference handbook from the University of Leeds Water Engineering series.

1. Uniform Flow Equations

Chézy Equation:
$$Q = A C \sqrt{R S_0} \quad \text{where } R = \frac{A}{P}$$
Manning's Formula:
$$Q = \frac{1}{n} A R^{2/3} S_0^{1/2}$$
Wide Rectangular Channel ($R \approx y$):
$$q = \frac{Q}{b} = \frac{1}{n} y_n^{5/3} S_0^{1/2} \implies y_n = \left(\frac{q n}{S_0^{1/2}}\right)^{3/5}$$

2. Critical Flow & Froude Number

General Cross-Section:
$$Fr = \frac{V}{\sqrt{g A / B}} = \frac{Q}{A\sqrt{g A / B}}, \quad Fr^2 = \frac{Q^2 B}{g A^3}$$
Rectangular Channel ($Fr = 1$):
$$y_c = \left(\frac{q^2}{g}\right)^{1/3}, \quad E_{sc} = \frac{3}{2}y_c \iff y_c = \frac{2}{3}E_{sc}$$
Critical Slope:
$$S_c = \frac{g n^2}{y_c^{1/3}}$$

3. Hydraulic Jump (Bélanger Equation)

Conjugate (Sequent) Depths:
$$y_2 = \frac{y_1}{2}\left(\sqrt{1 + 8Fr_1^2} - 1\right), \quad y_1 = \frac{y_2}{2}\left(\sqrt{1 + 8Fr_2^2} - 1\right)$$
Head Loss Across Jump:
$$\Delta E = E_1 - E_2 = \frac{(y_2 - y_1)^3}{4 y_1 y_2}$$
Power Dissipated:
$$P = \rho g Q \Delta E \quad \text{(Watts)}$$

4. Gradually Varied Flow (GVF)

Dynamic GVF Equation:
$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{S_0 - S_f}{1 - Fr^2} \quad \text{where } S_f = \frac{n^2 Q^2}{A^2 R^{4/3}}$$
Direct Step Method:
$$\Delta x = \frac{E_{s2} - E_{s1}}{S_0 - \frac{S_{f1} + S_{f2}}{2}}$$
