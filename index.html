Section E5: Relevant Equations of Steady Open Channel Flow

1. Uniform Flow

Chézy: $Q = A C \sqrt{R S_o}$ (Eq-1) | Manning: $Q = \frac{1}{n} A R^{2/3} S_o^{1/2}$ (Eq-2)

Wide Channel Normal Depth: $y_n = \left(\frac{q n}{S_o^{1/2}}\right)^{3/5} = \left(\frac{q}{C S_o^{1/2}}\right)^{2/3}$

2. Critical Conditions & Slope

Critical criterion: $\frac{Q^2 B}{g A^3} = 1$ | Rectangular: $y_c = \left(\frac{q^2}{g}\right)^{1/3}, \quad E_{sc} = \frac{3}{2} y_c$

Critical slope: $S_{oc} = \frac{g n^2 P_c}{B_c R_c^{1/3}}$, and for wide channels $S_{oc} = \frac{g n^2}{y_c^{1/3}}$

3. Hydraulic Jump

Sequent depth: $y_2 = \frac{y_1}{2}\left(\sqrt{1 + 8Fr_1^2} - 1\right)$ (Eq-39)

Energy dissipated: $\Delta E = \frac{(y_2 - y_1)^3}{4 y_1 y_2}$ (Eq-41) | Power: $P = \rho g Q \Delta E$

4. Gradually Varied Flow (GVF)

Dynamic equation: $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{S_o - S_f}{1 - Fr^2}$ (Eq-42)

Direct step: $\Delta x = \Delta y \left(\frac{1 - Fr^2}{S_o - S_f}\right)_{\text{mean}}$
